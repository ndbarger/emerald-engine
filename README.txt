designated for my own work rather than serving as a general-purpose
engine for everybody. You may use the engine for your own
games, but I'm not responsible for your usage.

engine is open source and free to use and modify, any projects
and data created that use the engine are still subject to their
own protection. Therefore, while you can modify and distribute the
engine's code, you cannot freely distribute data provided within

configuration, etc unless specified by its creator.

If you are curious about the specifics of the engine, please see
the docs folder.
